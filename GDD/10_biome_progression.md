# Biome Progression

## Muc Tieu

Biome la cach game mo dan the gioi, nhip hoc he thong va tang roleplay.

```text
Biome 1 = vuc day the luc trong vung khoi dau
Biome 2 = gap cac the luc khac va bat dau roleplay xa hoi
Biome 3+ = mo rong chien tranh, lien minh, di san va endgame
```

Biome khong chi la vung dia hinh. Moi biome la mot chuong roleplay, mot bo luat xa hoi, mot cau truc tai nguyen va mot nhip xung dot.

---

## Boi Canh Chung

Nguoi choi khoi dau trong mot Tieu Thien The Gioi do mot dai nang da toa hoa de lai.

Dai nang nay co the de lai cam che phong an tung biome, di san bi chia nho, linh mach suy tan, phap tac ky la, the luc ban dia bien dang va bi mat ve nguyen nhan toa hoa.

Moi lan vuot biome la mot lan nguoi choi pha them mot lop phong an/cam che va buoc vao tang xa hoi rong hon.

---

## Biome 1 - Can Co Va Vuc Day

Biome 1 la vung khoi dau tuong doi khep kin. No khong phai tutorial lo lieu, ma la chuong dau cua cau chuyen.

Nguoi choi can hieu vai lanh dao, cuu the luc khoi sup do, hoc khai thac tai nguyen, tao quyet dinh roleplay dau tien va mo duong sang biome 2.

| Thanh Phan | Chuc Nang |
|---|---|
| Can cu suy tan | trung tam khoi phuc the luc |
| Linh Mach Ha | nguon tai nguyen nen |
| Di tich cua dai nang | lore, co duyen, objective |
| Faction ban dia nho | NPC de giao dich, cuu, doi dau hoac thu phuc |
| Cam che bien gioi | ly do chua sang biome 2 ngay |
| Co duyen ca nhan | build identity dau season |

### Dieu Kien Vuot Biome 1

Khong nen chi la dat level X. Nen la to hop: can cu dat nguong, co doi quan/tuong du on, giai quyet crisis dau tien, chon di san/dinh huong, mo hoac pha cam che sang biome 2.

| Vai | Dieu Kien Vuot Biome 1 |
|---|---|
| Tong Chu | noi lai linh mach + to chuc phap hoi dau tien |
| Hoang De | binh dinh phan loan + on dinh dan/luong |
| Bang Chu | gianh lai dia ban chinh + xu ly noi gian |
| Thuong Chu | mo lai tuyen buon + tra/doi mon no lon |
| Phap Chu | xu xong an cu + lap lai hieu luc an tin |

## Biome 2 - Roleplay Giua Cac Ben

Biome 2 la noi game that su mo xa hoi. Nhieu nguoi choi/the luc bat dau cung hien dien tren map.

Tai day, roleplay phai co dat song: loi hua co gia tri, phan boi co hau qua, giao thuong can niem tin, chien tranh can ly do, danh tieng anh huong hanh vi NPC/nguoi choi.

| Thanh Phan | Chuc Nang |
|---|---|
| Vung trung lap | noi gap nhau, giao thuong, xung dot |
| Resource node hiem | ly do tranh chap |
| Choke point | tao chien thuat map kieu Heroes/Jebus |
| Cho/diem giao dich | ly do roleplay kinh te |
| Cong trinh cong cong | objective chung, de sinh lien minh/phan boi |
| Linh Mach Trung | nang cap quan/can cu, buoc va cham |
| NPC faction lon hon | the luc thu ba lam trong tai hoac ke pha game |

Truc roleplay chinh:

```text
Ngoai giao -> Hiep uoc -> Giao thuong -> Tranh chap -> Chien tranh -> Hau qua -> Lich su server
```

Biome 2 can luu lai lich su: ai giup ai, ai phan boi, ai cuop mo, ai cuu vien, ai pha hiep uoc, ai thang tran lon.

## Biome 3+ - Mo Rong Va Di San

Chua chot chi tiet. Dinh huong: mo map lon hon, nhieu biome co phap tac rieng, chien tranh lien cum, di san dai nang lo ro, endgame lien quan quyen kiem soat Tieu Thien The Gioi.
