# He Thong The Luc Va Nganh Nghe

## Nguyen Tac Cot Loi

Game khong bat dau bang viec chon mot class co dinh. Nguoi choi chon **than phan lanh dao** cua mot the luc suy tan, sau do dung the luc do buoc vao he sinh thai roleplay lon hon.

~~~text
Than phan khoi dau -> vuc day the luc -> vuot Biome 1 -> chon/chiem/phuc dung nganh nghe Biome 2
~~~

Nguyen tac:

- Khong co nghe nao chi la support bi dong; moi nghe phai co thu ma nguoi khac can.
- Khong khoa content bang role; role chi lam thay doi hieu suat, chi phi, toc do va rui ro.
- Cang chuyen sau mot nghe, nguoi choi cang manh o vung do nhung cang phu thuoc vao he sinh thai.
- Tai nguyen khong chi tu sinh; tang truong lon phai den tu tranh doat, giao thuong, bao ke, hop dong va quan he.
- Map phai tao ly do de cac role gap nhau, can nhau, so nhau va doi khi phan boi nhau.

## Khoi Dau: 5 Than Phan Lanh Dao

Nguoi choi khoi dau trong Biome 1 bang mot trong 5 kieu the luc nen. Day la identity chinh tri/xa hoi ban dau, khong phai nghe nghiep cuoi cung.

| Role khoi dau | Fantasy | Loi the nen | Han che nen |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hoang Trieu | Vuc lai trieu dinh suy vi | Dan dong, tap quyen, chuyen dan thanh quan nhanh | Hau can nang, co may cong kenh, de bi tri tre boi noi bo |
| Tong Mon | Khoi phuc tong mon mat truyen thua | De tu tinh anh, hoc nhanh, hop phap mon cao | Dau vao khat khe, kho mo rong dai tra |
| Hoc Vien | Dung lai noi dao tao va nghien cuu | Chuan hoa dao tao, mo khoa kien thuc on dinh | It bung no so luong, can thoi gian va thiet bi |
| The Gia | Cuu gia toc/huyet mach khoi diet vong | Noi toc ben, tuong chat luong, quan he gia toc | Phu thuoc huyet mach, hon nhan, danh vong |
| Bang Phai | Gom nguoi, chiem dia ban, song bang nghia khi | Linh hoat, chiem co hoi nhanh, hop viec ngam | Ky luat thap, kho chuyen hoa hang loat |

Than phan khong dong nghia voi tinh cach. Cung la Hoang Trieu, nguoi choi co the dien minh quan, bao quan, nhiep chinh, hoang tu phuc quoc hoac chua hau cat cu.

## Biome 2: Nganh Nghe Song

Sau khi vuot Biome 1, nguoi choi buoc vao Biome 2, noi cac the luc gap nhau. Luc nay trong tam khong con la "toi tu farm du moi thu", ma la chon cach song trong mot xa hoi co thieu hut.

Nganh nghe Biome 2 co 4 lop:

| Lop | Y nghia |
|---|---|
| Di chi truyen thua | Dia diem tren map, co the bi chiem/cuop, mo duong phuc dung nganh |
| Kien thuc phuc dung | Thu da hoc duoc khi xay dung/nghien cuu; mat dia diem khong mat sach kien thuc |
| Co so van hanh | Cong trinh, nhan luc, vat tu, tuyen van tai de bien kien thuc thanh san pham/quan |
| Mang quan he | Hop dong, bao ke, giao thuong, lien minh, thue chien dau, tin dung, uy tin |

Chiem nhieu Di Chi khong co nghia la du nang luc phuc dung tat ca. Nguoi choi co the giu dia diem, nhung neu thieu nhan luc, vat tu, ky su, phap ly hoac bao ve thi di chi chi la tai san chua khai thac het.

## 8 Nhom Nganh Chinh

### 1. Chien Dau

Vai tro: danh boss, ho tong, chiem node, bao ve thuong doi, lam nhiem vu truyen thua, danh thue.

| Nganh vi du | San pham/dich vu | Can tu nguoi khac | Tao drama |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kiem Tu | Don vi don dau, chem tuong, pha boss | Vu khi, dan duoc, thong tin muc tieu | Thue danh boss, tranh cong, doi tien thuong |
| Luyen The | Tank, xung phong, giu tuyen | Dan duoc hoi phuc, giap, hau can | Lam la chan cho phe yeu, doi quyen loi |
| Phap Thuat chien | AOE, cong thanh, burst chien truong | Linh thach, phap khi, phu luc | Bi tranh gianh trong chien tranh lon |
| Danh thue | Luc luong hop dong | Tien, phap ly, uy tin | Lat keo, bo keo, bi mua chuoc |

Diem manh: giai quyet viec map nhanh. Diem yeu: an tai nguyen nhieu, phu thuoc hau can va thong tin.

### 2. Cong Trinh Va Tran Phap

Vai tro: xay thanh, dung ket gioi, phuc dung di chi, mo khoa co so tier cao, bien dia diem thanh he thong van hanh.

| Nganh vi du | San pham/dich vu | Can tu nguoi khac | Tao drama |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tran Phap | Quan tran, ket gioi, dia loi | Vat lieu, phu luc, nhan luc | Ai duoc vao tran, ai bi chan ngoai cong |
| Ky Su di chi | Ban thiet ke, sua co so, nang cap cong trinh | Luyen Khi, Thuong Hoi, bao ve | Bi bat coc, bi tranh nhan su |
| Cong thanh/thu thanh | Cong cu pha thanh, tuong thanh, thap phong ngu | Quang, go da, linh thach | Chay dua vu trang giua lang gieng |

Diem manh: bien tai nguyen thanh loi the dai han. Diem yeu: cham, can nhieu dau vao, de thanh muc tieu bi pha hoai.

### 3. Luyen Che

Vai tro: tao vu khi, phap bao, phu luc, cong cu, may moc va vat pham phuc dung di chi.

| Nganh vi du | San pham/dich vu | Can tu nguoi khac | Tao drama |
|---|---|---|---|
| Luyen Khi | Vu khi, giap, phap bao, may cong thanh | Quang, lo luyen, ban ve, van tai | Ban vu khi cho ai, cam van ai |
| Phu Luc | Phu phong an, buff, debuff, trap | Vat lieu dac thu, tri thuc, ky su | False flag, phu gia, phu cam duong |
| Co Quan | May moc, cong cu khai thac, thiet bi phuc dung | Luyen Khi, Tran Phap, linh thach | Doc quyen cong nghe, bi danh cap ban ve |

Diem manh: la dau vao cua rat nhieu nganh. Diem yeu: phu thuoc nguon vat lieu va duong van tai.

### 4. Dan Duoc Va Sinh Ton

Vai tro: hoi phuc, dot pha, tang san luong, giam hao hut, duy tri chien tranh dai ngay.

| Nganh vi du | San pham/dich vu | Can tu nguoi khac | Tao drama |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dan Su | Dan hoi phuc, dot pha, doc dan, dan dan sinh | Linh thao, lo dan, bao ve, thi truong | Dau co dan, ban dan cho hai phe |
| Duoc Vien | Trong linh thao, xu ly doc, cung ung on dinh | Dat tot, nuoc, nhan cong, bao ve | Tranh vung linh thao, dot vuon thuoc |
| Y Su chien truong | Giam hao ton sau tran, cuu tuong/NPC | Dan duoc, bao ve, van tai | Cuu ai truoc, bo ai lai |

Diem manh: co the tu tuc song tot va ban san pham lien tuc. Diem yeu: yeu tranh dau, de bi ep bao ke neu thieu chien luc.

### 5. Thuong Mai Va Hau Can

Vai tro: van tai, kho, tin dung, cho, bao hiem, cung ung lien minh, ket noi nhu cau giua cac nganh.

| Nganh vi du | San pham/dich vu | Can tu nguoi khac | Tao drama |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thuong Hoi | Cho, hop dong, dau gia, mua ban vat pham | An ninh, phap ly, uy tin | Cam van, thao tung gia, no xau |
| Hau Can | Kho, xe hang, tuyen tiep te, nuoi quan | Duong an toan, bao ve, linh thach | Bi chan duong, thu phi, cuop hang |
| Ngan kho/tin dung | Cho vay, bao lanh, doi tien | Phap Gia, uy tin, du lieu giao dich | Vo no, thu hoi no, chien tranh tai chinh |

Diem manh: ai cung can. Diem yeu: gia tri nam tren duong di va uy tin, nen bi tan cong bang cuop, tin don va phap ly.

### 6. Tinh Bao Va Ngam

Vai tro: scout, ban thong tin, cai nguoi, pha hoai, false flag, tao drama co du lieu.

| Nganh vi du | San pham/dich vu | Can tu nguoi khac | Tao drama |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mat Tham | Bao cao fog, thong tin hanh quan, danh sach kho | Tien, che chan phap ly, noi ung | Ban thong tin hai dau, lo noi gian |
| Am Tu | Am sat NPC, pha hoai co so, bat coc ky su | Danh tinh gia, duong rut, doc/khi cu | Ai thue? ai bi do toi? |
| Tong Thien Nhan | He thong ghi nhan bien dong, goi y drama, mang tin don | Du lieu, AI tro ly, nguon tin | Tin that/tin gia, thao tung du luan |

Diem manh: bien su khong chac chan thanh hang hoa. Diem yeu: neu mat uy tin hoac bi lo danh tinh thi bi ca server san lung.

### 7. Ngoai Giao Va Phap Ly

Vai tro: hop dong, hiep uoc, trong tai, truy na, bao lanh, cong nhan quyen so huu va tao he qua cho phan boi.

| Nganh vi du | San pham/dich vu | Can tu nguoi khac | Tao drama |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phap Gia | Hop dong, truy na, xac nhan giao dich, bao chung | Uy tin, luc cuong che, du lieu | Luat le co bi mua chuoc khong |
| Ngoai Giao Quan | Dam phan, lien minh, chia loot, ngung chien | Qua tang, thong tin, uy danh | Hua mieng, mat mat, bo phieu lien minh |
| Le Nghiep/danh vong | Sac phong, nghi le, cong nhan chinh danh | Tai nguyen, thua nhan xa hoi | Ai duoc goi la chinh thong |

Diem manh: lam loi hua co gia tri that. Diem yeu: neu khong co luc cuong che hoac uy tin, luat chi la giay.

### 8. Ho Tro Dac Thu Va Moi Truong

Vai tro: tao vung song dac biet, thu phuc linh thu, trong linh moc, khai hoang, tao nguon nguyen lieu hiem.

| Nganh vi du | San pham/dich vu | Can tu nguoi khac | Tao drama |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ngu Thu | Linh thu, ky thu, scout hoang da, quai thu chien | Dan duoc, thuc an, bai san, bao ve | Linh thu thoat, bi tranh bat |
| Linh Moc | Linh thao, go linh, vung sinh truong | Dat, nuoc, tran phap, nhan luc | Tranh dat tot, dot rung, bao ke vuon |
| Khai Hoang | Mo duong, mo node, bien vung xam thanh tai san | Chien dau, cong trinh, hau can | Ai so huu vung moi mo |

Diem manh: tao nguon dau vao hiem. Diem yeu: can dat/vung, nen gan truc tiep voi tranh chap lanh tho.

## Co Che Tai Nguyen Tao Quan He

Moi nganh nen co 3 tang nhu cau:

| Tang nhu cau | Nguon co | Tac dung thiet ke |
|---|---|---|
| Song toi thieu | Tu sinh trong can cu/node nho | Cho nguoi choi solo van song duoc |
| Tang truong tot | Giao thuong, hop dong, di chi, tuyen van tai | Bat dau can nguoi khac |
| Dot pha/tier cao | Chiem giu, tranh doat, boss, linh mach, vat pham hiem | Tao chien tranh, lien minh, drama |

Nguyen tac can bang: tu sinh giup song, nhung khong du de thang cuoc dua. Tranh doat giup dot pha, nhung neu qua nhieu se thanh chaos. Thuong mai va phap ly la lop boi tron de xung dot co cau chuyen thay vi chi la giet nhau.

## Vi Du Chuoi Roleplay

### Dan Duoc muon len tier cao

1. Dan Su co cong thuc nhung thieu linh thao hiem.
2. Linh Moc biet vung moc linh thao nhung can nguoi bao ve.
3. Chien Dau duoc thue danh boss giu vung.
4. Thuong Hoi van chuyen lo hang qua choke dang bi Bang Phai thu phi.
5. Phap Gia lap hop dong chia loi nhuan va dieu khoan bao ve.
6. Mat Tham ban tin co phe thu ba sap cuop hang.
7. Neu mot ben phan boi, lich su server ghi lai va mo ra drama truy na/tra thu.

### Tran Phap phuc dung thanh tri

1. Hoang Trieu chiem duoc Di Chi Tran Phap nhung thieu phu luc va ky su.
2. Hoc Vien co ky su, Phu Luc co vat pham, Luyen Khi co cong cu.
3. Thuong Hoi gom nguyen lieu, Chien Dau giu vung cong truong.
4. Neu thanh tri bi cuop, Hoang Trieu mat quyen mua quan tai do, nhung kien thuc da phuc dung van con.
5. Phe cuop phai tu xay mang van hanh moi, khong tu dong huong 100% gia tri.
