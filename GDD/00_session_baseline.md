# Ban Chuan Ket Thuc Phien Hom Nay

Tai lieu nay la moc chuan de tiep tuc GDD sau phien lam viec hom nay. Neu cac file cu co noi dung lech voi ban nay, uu tien ban nay lam nguon tham chieu.

---

## 1. Vision Chinh

**Manh Vo Dai Thien** la game **PC-first roleplay strategy** trong boi canh tien hiep.

Nguoi choi khong phai mot ca nhan vo danh di farm quai. Nguoi choi la nguoi lanh dao hoac ke thua mot the luc suy tan, phai vuc day the luc do tu mot tieu thien the gioi rieng, sau do buoc vao dai the gioi noi cac the luc khac cung ton tai.

Mobile co ton tai, nhung chi la lop phu tro: doc bao cao, quan ly tai nguyen nho, dat lenh dieu kien, xu ly thao tac nen khi khong ngoi PC. Mobile/AFK/Be Quan khong duoc tro thanh core thang thua.

---

## 5. Biome 1 Chuan

Biome 1 la **tieu thien the gioi rieng cua tung nguoi choi**, hinh thanh tu co the mot dai nang co xua da toa hoa.

Muc dich:

- mo dau cau chuyen va than phan lanh dao
- day nguoi choi hoc he thong nhanh, khong tutorial dai
- chi cap tai nguyen thap: go, da, luong thuc, linh thach cap thap
- chi cho xay nen co ban va quan tier 1-2
- day nguoi choi ra dai the gioi vi Biome 1 se can tai nguyen

Cong ra Biome 1:

- la thi luyen cua dai nang
- duoc nguy trang thanh dan quai vat can cong kieu Jebus Outcast
- moi cong/boss co cot truyen rieng
- danh xong boss la duoc di ngay
- boss phu co the co reward/lore/duong tat, nhung khong bat buoc

## 6. Biome 2 Chuan

Biome 2 la noi game chuyen tu khoi phuc noi bo sang **xa hoi song**.

Biome 2 phai co:

- nhieu nguoi choi/the luc cung ton tai
- tai nguyen khong du cho tat ca
- Di Chi Truyen Thua co the chiem, cuop, mat quyen khai thac
- choke point, tuyen giao thuong, cho trung lap, Linh Mach, vung xam
- luat ngoai giao, reputation, hiep uoc va phan boi co hau qua
- roleplay giua cac than phan lanh dao va cac nganh nghe

Nguyen tac quan trong:

~~~text
Tu sinh giup song -> giao thuong giup phat trien -> tranh doat/Di Chi giup dot pha
~~~

Neu chi tu sinh, game thanh idle. Neu chi tranh doat, game thanh chaos. Biome 2 can ca hai, voi thuong mai va phap ly lam lop boi tron drama.

## 8. Di Chi Truyen Thua

Di Chi Truyen Thua la trung tam cua Biome 2 va quan tier cao.

Di Chi khong chi la mot building. No gom 4 lop:

| Lop | Y nghia |
|---|---|
| Dia diem | Node tren map co the chiem, cuop, phong toa. |
| Kien thuc phuc dung | Thu da hoc duoc; mat dia diem khong mat sach kien thuc. |
| Co so van hanh | Cong trinh, nhan luc, vat tu, tuyen van tai de bien tri thuc thanh san pham/quan. |
| Mang quan he | Hop dong, bao ke, giao thuong, lien minh, phap ly, uy tin. |

Chiem nhieu Di Chi khong co nghia du nang luc phuc dung tat ca. Neu thieu ky su, vat tu, nhan luc, phap ly, van tai hoac bao ve, Di Chi chi la tai san chua khai thac het.

Neu bi cuop:

- mat quyen mua/khai thac hien tai tai Di Chi do
- quan da mua van con
- buff/training tu Di Chi co the mat den khi chiem lai hoac tim nguon thay the
- kien thuc da phuc dung va cong trinh da hoc khong mat sach

## 10. AI Ngon Tay Vang Va Tong Thien Nhan

AI Ngon Tay Vang la tro ly cua nguoi choi, khong phai he thong tu choi thay.

Vai tro:

- tom tat bao cao khi nguoi choi quay lai
- goi y rui ro, co hoi, bien dong xa hoi
- ho tro cac role can tuong tac cao nhu Mat Tham, Ngoai Giao, Phap Gia, Thuong Mai
- ket noi voi Tong Thien Nhan de tao drama co du lieu: tin don, lich su bien dong, dau vet hanh quan, giao dich, phan boi

Tong Thien Nhan khong nen la camera toan tri lo lieu. No nen tao thong tin co do tin cay, co nguon, co the bi thao tung va co gia tri roleplay.
